# Privacy Policy – MeiHome

MeiHome is an Android dashboard application for personal and household use.

## Data collection and use
MeiHome does not use advertising or analytics and does not sell personal data.

The application is intended to use Google Firebase services to provide its cloud features:

- Firebase Authentication / Google Sign-In: to identify the signed-in user
- Firebase Cloud Firestore: to access and synchronize household and list data belonging to the user
- Firebase Crashlytics: to receive technical crash reports and diagnose app errors

Depending on the features used, data processed through Firebase may include the user's account identifier and email address, list and household content created or shared by users, and technical crash information such as device and operating-system information, app state, stack traces, and diagnostic identifiers.

Google may process technical information required to provide these services. For information about Google's handling of data, see Google's privacy policy and Firebase privacy and security documentation.

## Local data
App settings and configuration may be stored locally on the device.

## Third parties
MeiHome does not contain advertising SDKs and does not use Firebase Analytics or other analytics/tracking services.

Google services used by the application are subject to Google's privacy terms:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Firebase Privacy and Security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy/

## Data sharing
Personal data is not sold. Data is transmitted to Google/Firebase only where required to provide the cloud, authentication, and crash-reporting functions described above.

## Data deletion
Data stored locally can be removed by clearing the app data or uninstalling the application. Cloud data associated with MeiHome can be deleted on request using the contact address below, subject to data that is shared with other users and must remain available to them.

## Security
Reasonable technical measures are used to protect data. No method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

## Children
MeiHome is not specifically directed at children.

## Changes
This privacy policy may be updated when the application's functionality or services change. The current version is published in this repository.
